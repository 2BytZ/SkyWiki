This website is created for the purpose of writing down my own personal headcanons for my player character in the Elder Scrolls series.

It serves the purpose of acting like a real wiki, with links and references to things that actually happened in the game, and using that to further reinforce my characters' "backstory" and, well, their character!

Currently, the site is still very much under construction and it is still missing a lot of content and material that I either haven't started to even write yet, or just haven't inserted into the website yet. The most full and up-to-date page on the website currently is Dranleic Haligdrake, who is my first Skyrim playthrough character.

I had a lot of fun making this website and I will continue to improve upon it and add more content whenever I come around to it. Until then, I hope you too can find some joy in what I've created out of my passion for this game series, and even inspire you for a character in your next playthrough ;)
